# Sprint 3 traceability: SPM-82 Request a Replacement Venue for a Disrupted Booking

Source: Jira export `SPM List - Jira (2).csv` (12 October 2026) and the Week 7 customer change "Venue Becomes Unavailable After Booking" (affected bookings are identified and the coordinator can search for and request a replacement venue, preserving the original event information). The nine acceptance criteria below are the agreed refinement of the Jira text (same intent; permission and one-pending-replacement rules made explicit). Implementation and local test references only; shared-environment UAT has not been run.

Design: a replacement is a new booking linked to the disrupted one by `replaces_booking_id`. Both are active until Venue Staff approve the replacement, so the one-active-booking-per-event rule ignores replacements and a unique index allows one active replacement per booking. The period, expected attendance (from the event) and main-venue flag are copied; approval cancels only the replaced booking.

## Compatibility with other stories

- **SPM-83:** consumes the disruption flag; approval clears it (so the row shows Resolved and ages out of the list after 14 days). A pending replacement stays pending if the block is removed meanwhile, and approving it still cancels the original (pgTAP).
- **SPM-24/25:** ordinary requests and approvals are unchanged; the one-active-booking rule still refuses a normal request while any booking is active (pgTAP regression on the ordinary approval wording and effect).
- **SPM-85:** adds `is_main_venue` (default true) for it to use; no other multi-venue rules. Only the replaced booking is cancelled.
- **SPM-33 / SPM-90 / SPM-38:** Confirmed-to-Planning is tested only by setting the status in pgTAP; date and time changes are refused here and left to SPM-38.

## Known limits

- Migrations `202610140001`, `202610140002` and `202610140003` have not been applied to the shared environment.
- `database.types.ts` was edited by hand for the new columns and function; regenerate it once the migrations are applied.
- Suitability for a replacement: capacity and required facilities are hard checks (the brief names both as the cases where a venue should not normally be treated as suitable); layout and accessibility are guidance only (search filters in the brief, reviewed again by the Safety Officer) and are shown to Venue Staff as "Check before deciding". Ordinary (non-replacement) requests are unchanged and still do not block on either.
- The organiser is not notified of the venue change (out of scope; belongs with the notification stories).
